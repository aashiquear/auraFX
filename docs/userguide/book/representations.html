auraFX User Guide

Volumes, point clouds and curved cells

Representations

Representation in Properties (and the toolbar) chooses how an object is drawn:

RepresentationUse
Surfacethe outer surface, smooth-shaded (hard edges stay sharp: a 30° crease angle)
Surface With Edgesthe surface with cell edges (Edge color in Properties, edge_color= in Python)
Wireframeedges only
Pointsthe points, as flat dots or shaded spheres (Point size, spheres)
Outlinethe bounding box (every box of AMR data)
VolumeGPU ray casting of image data (other meshes are resampled first)
Surface LICa vector field as streaks (see Lighting)
Moleculeatoms as spheres and bonds as cylinders (see Molecules)

Lighting can be switched off per object (flat colors, for 2-D data). Axes grid draws a labelled grid around the object with tick values.

Line width (Properties › Display, shown for Wireframe, Surface With Edges, Outline and line data; line_width= in Python) sets how many pixels wide lines, wireframes and cell edges are drawn, from 1 to 10 (any width from Python). It is a width on the screen, so lines keep their width when you zoom. Wider lines are drawn as flat bands with square ends (reaching half the width past each point), which fills the joints of bent lines. Wide lines show in screenshots and in pictures drawn on a server; picking still uses 1-pixel lines. For lines with a thickness in the scene (shaded, and growing as you zoom in), use the Tube filter.

c.set(representation="Wireframe", line_width=2)
w.set(representation="Outline", line_width=6)

Volume rendering

The Volume representation draws image data as a translucent cloud colored by the array: Volume density sets how opaque it is and Transparent below makes values under a fraction of the range invisible, which clears away the noise around a feature. Volumes are depth-merged with the surfaces around them.

w.set(representation="Volume", color_by="RTData", volume_opacity=2.0, volume_threshold=0.3)

Point clouds and particles

LAMMPS dumps, XYZ files, PDB files and point sources open as points. Draw them as spheres sized by Point size, color them by type, and switch on Eye-Dome Lighting to see the structure of a dense cloud. The Glyph filter draws arrows or spheres scaled by an array; Tube gives lines a thickness.

Molecules

Molecules are points (the atoms) with an atomic number array (atomic_number, or VTK’s Atomic Numbers), and lines (the bonds). The PDB, XYZ, VASP and CML readers produce them (and the atoms block of a Gaussian cube file). A molecule opens in the Molecule representation, colored by element with the Elements color map (Jmol’s colors). Files with more than 100 000 atoms open as points instead; switch to Molecule when you need it.

Settings (Properties › Display, or node.set(...)):

SettingPythonDefaultWhat it does
Stylemolecule_styleBall and StickBall and Stick: atoms at a fraction of their van der Waals radius, bonds as thin sticks. Van der Waals: atoms at their full radius, no bonds (space filling). Liquorice: atoms and bonds as tubes of one width
Atom sizeatom_scale0.3fraction of the van der Waals radius (Ball and Stick)
Bond radiusbond_radius0.075 Åradius of the sticks
Bond ordersbond_ordersondouble and triple bonds (a bond_order array, from CML files) drawn as two or three sticks

Each half of a bond takes the color of its atom. Color by any other atom array (charge, residue, B factor…) with Color by as usual. The radii and colors come from VTK’s periodic table.

m = afx.load("molecule.cml")                 # Molecule representation, colored by element
m.set(molecule_style="Van der Waals")        # or "Liquorice", "Ball and Stick"
m.set(atom_scale=0.25, bond_radius=0.1, bond_orders=False)
b = m.compute_molecule_bonds(tolerance=0.45) # bonds from distances

Filters › Compute Molecule Bonds finds bonds from distances: two atoms are bonded when they are closer than the sum of their covalent radii, each increased by half the Tolerance (0.45 Å). With Absolute tolerance off, each radius is multiplied by the tolerance instead. Bonds already there stay; only new ones are added. The bonds were verified on a test molecule with each tolerance setting.

Large structures (crystals, MD snapshots) draw fastest as Points with spheres. The Molecule representation builds geometry for every atom, which is fine up to a few hundred thousand atoms.

Curved cells

Quadratic cells and Lagrange or Bezier cells of any order (VTK types 68–80, with rational weights and per-cell degrees) are drawn curved. Curved cells › subdivision in Properties sets how finely: 0 draws straight edges between corners, 1 passes through the nodes, each further level halves the pieces. Slice, Clip, Contour, Probe and Integrate Variables follow the curved shape; the Tessellate filter turns curved cells into straight ones for filters that need them. The Information panel lists the cell types.

a.set(nonlinear_subdivision=3)
t = a.tessellate(level=2)
